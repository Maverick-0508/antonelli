Understand the differences and choose the right home charging setup for your needs.

Level 1 Charging

  • Uses a standard 120V household outlet.
  • Typically provides 2-5 miles of range per hour of charging.
  • Best for overnight charging and low daily mileage drivers.
  • Usually requires no special installation—just plug in your EV’s charging cable.

Level 2 Charging

  • Uses a 240V outlet (like those for dryers or ovens).
  • Provides 10-30+ miles of range per hour of charging.
  • Ideal for faster charging and households with multiple EVs or higher daily mileage.
  • Requires professional installation by a licensed electrician.

Installation Tips

  • Consult a certified electrician to assess your home’s electrical system.
  • Choose a charging station that is compatible with your EV and future needs.
  • Consider smart chargers for scheduling and energy monitoring.
  • Check for local incentives or rebates for EV charger installations.
