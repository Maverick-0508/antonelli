EV Charging Guide 2026

Understand the different charging options, speeds, and tips for efficient charging.

Home Charging & Installations
Key points: Level 1, Level 2, and DC Fast Charging each offer different speeds. Most daily charging is done at home, but public networks are growing.
Charger Type Speed Connector Where Used Best For
Level 1 3–5 miles/hour
(8–20 hours for full charge)		 Standard 3-pin plug (UK/US) Home (regular outlet) Overnight charging, low daily mileage
Level 2 15–30 miles/hour
(4–8 hours for full charge)		 Type 2 (UK/EU), J1772 (US) Home (dedicated unit), public stations Faster home charging, most public charging
DC Fast Charging 100–300+ miles/hour
(20–60 min for 80%)		 CCS, CHAdeMO, Tesla Supercharger Public rapid charging stations Long trips, quick top-ups

How to Charge at Home

  1. Plug into a standard outlet (Level 1) or install a dedicated wallbox (Level 2).
  2. Charge overnight for convenience and lower electricity rates.
  3. Use a timer or app to schedule charging if available.
  4. Keep battery between 20–80% for best health.

How to Use Public Chargers

  1. Find a charger using apps (Zap-Map, PlugShare, Google Maps).
  2. Check connector type and payment method.
  3. Plug in, follow on-screen instructions, and monitor charging.
  4. Unplug and move your car when finished to free the space.
Charging Tips:
  • Precondition your car (heat/cool) while plugged in to save battery.
  • Fast charging is convenient but can accelerate battery wear if used exclusively.
  • Public charging costs vary—check rates before plugging in.
  • Always check your car’s manual for charging recommendations.
