Making zero emission vehicles more affordable and supporting a greener future.

What is the Electric Car Grant?

The Electric Car Grant is a government initiative to help drivers purchase new zero emission cars priced at or under £37,000. The grant is available from 16 July 2025 and is funded until the 2028-2029 financial year (subject to change or early closure if funds run out).

Latest update (15 July 2025): The UK government has launched a new Electric Car Grant to make zero emission vehicles more affordable and support sustainable manufacturing.

How much is the grant?

  1. £3,750 for the most sustainably produced cars
  2. £1,500 for cars that meet some environmental criteria

The grant amount depends on the sustainability of the vehicle's production. Cars must meet strict environmental standards to qualify.

Who is eligible?

  1. New zero emission cars priced at or under £37,000
  2. Vehicle must be on the Department for Transport's approved list
  3. Manufacturers must meet minimum sustainability standards (science-based targets)

How does sustainability affect the grant?

  • To qualify for the higher grant (£3,750), vehicles must be produced to the highest sustainability standards, including low carbon emissions during manufacturing and battery production.
  • All eligible vehicles must be produced by manufacturers with verified science-based targets for reducing environmental impact.
  • Grant amount is based on the carbon intensity of the electricity grid where the vehicle and battery are assembled.

How to apply

  1. Car manufacturers apply for vehicle eligibility. See application details.
  2. Consumers can check the list of eligible vehicles before purchasing.
  3. Grant is applied at the point of sale by the dealership.

Other government support

  • £25 million for cross-pavement charging channels
  • £30 million for chargepoints at depots for vans, coaches, and HGVs
  • £8 million for chargers at NHS sites
  • New rules to allow EV hubs to be signed from major roads
  • Over £6 billion in private investment to expand the UK’s chargepoint network by 2030
Note: The scheme may close early or change without notice if funds are exhausted. Always check the official government page for the latest information.
