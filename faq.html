Charging & Installation

Level 1 uses a standard 120V household outlet and adds about 3–5 miles of range per hour. Level 2 uses a dedicated 240V wallbox and adds 25–30 miles per hour — ideal for overnight home charging. See our Installations page for details.

Vehicles & Environment

Yes. EVs produce zero tailpipe emissions and are up to 89% more energy efficient than combustion engines. Over their lifetime, they produce significantly less greenhouse gas — even accounting for battery manufacturing. Learn more on our All About Electric Cars page.

The UK government offers grants for new EVs, home charger installations, and zero-emission vehicle exemptions. Eligibility varies — see our EV Incentives page for a full breakdown of current schemes.

Most EV batteries are designed to last 8–15 years. Battery degradation is gradual — most retain 80–90% capacity after 8 years. Proper charging habits and thermal management help extend lifespan. Read more in our EV Battery Basics guide.

Yes. EVs have far fewer moving parts than combustion vehicles — no oil changes, no transmission fluid, fewer brake replacements (thanks to regenerative braking). The main maintenance areas are tyres, brakes, and battery health checks. We offer EV maintenance services for all vehicle types.
